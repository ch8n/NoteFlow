# Product Definition

## Product Name
NoteFlow

## Problem Statement
Students struggle to convert unstructured spoken content from video transcriptions into organized, actionable study notes that enhance comprehension and retention.

## Target Users
Students who consume educational content through video transcriptions and need structured, easy-to-review material for effective learning.

## Core Features
- Share Video Transcription URLs directly from device via app sharing functionality  
- Automatically extract audio transcripts with accurate timing information
- Convert transcriptions into structured study notes with key points, bullet lists, and summaries using AI
- Store generated notes in RoomDB with the original URL as identifier
- Search for study notes by URL or content

## Value Proposition
Our product provides students with an automated way to transform unstructured video transcriptions into organized, actionable study notes. This significantly improves comprehension, retention, and learning efficiency by turning passive audio consumption into active, structured study material.

## Success Metrics
- Number of Video Transcriptions processed per week  
- Average time from video sharing to study note availability 
- User engagement metrics (time spent viewing notes, frequency of use) 
- User satisfaction score (via post-usage survey) 
- Error rate in transcription accuracy (measured against manual transcription)
